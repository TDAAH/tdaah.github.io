Hamster Oracle

Board game rules questions, answered from the rulebook and cited to the page.

Stop flipping through the rulebook mid-game. Pick your game, ask a rules question in plain words, and get a short answer with the rulebook page it came from, so the table can check it and get back to playing.

What it does

  • Cited answers: every answer points to the rulebook page and quotes it. If the rulebook doesn't cover something, the app says so instead of guessing.
  • Photo questions: take a picture of a card or the board and ask about it.
  • Game aids: setup checklists, quick references and scoring sheets built from the rulebook.
  • Table tools: dice roller, timers, first-player picker, counters and a score tracker.
  • Play log: keep track of what you played and the scores.
