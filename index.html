Who we are

More Than Just a Game

At Two Dudes and a Hamster, we believe the best games do more than entertain.

We want to create experiences that make people laugh, think, question, and connect. Whether we're exploring education, ethics, morality, politics, pop culture, or the fascinating differences between cultures, our goal is to turn interesting ideas into unforgettable experiences.

We believe meaningful questions don't always need serious settings. Sometimes, the best conversations start with a ridiculous card, an unexpected answer, or a little friendly competition.

Make it fun. Make it memorable. Make it meaningful.

What We're Creating

From ridiculous party games to helpful tabletop tools, we're building experiences designed to make game night better.

Side Effects Are WHAT?!

The party game for the medically unqualified.

Think you've heard of some strange medical side effects? Think again.

Side Effects Are WHAT?! is a hilarious party card game where players face ridiculous medical scenarios and compete to come up with the funniest answers.

It's fast, absurd, and perfect for game nights with friends.

  • 3–8 Players
  • Ages 14+
  • Approximately 15 Minutes

Hamster Oracle

Tiny brain. Infinite wisdom.

Every board game group has that moment: someone asks a rules question, the game stops, and everyone starts digging through the rulebook.

Hamster Oracle helps you get back to playing.

Ask a question in plain English and get a quick answer supported by the game's rulebook, including the relevant page reference.

It's your pocket-sized rules assistant for game night.
