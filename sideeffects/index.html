Side Effects Are WHAT?!

The Cure Might Be Worse Than the Condition.

A ridiculous party game where questionable treatments meet even more questionable side effects. Choose your cure, accept the consequences, and find out what your friends are willing to risk.

  • 3–8 Players
  • 14+ Ages
  • 15 Minutes

Preparing for Kickstarter

Prescribe a Cure. Make It Worse.

Every round begins with a condition that needs treating.

Players suggest their treatments, then everyone gets a chance to make those treatments considerably less appealing.

Would you take a treatment if it caused hallucinations? What about accelerated balding? Or the unexpected horror of being actually content with life?

Once the side effects are revealed, everyone votes for the treatment they'd choose.

The results are rarely what you'd expect.

How to Play

Five simple steps. One very questionable cure.

Step 1 of 5

Reveal the Condition

The Orator flips over a Condition card. This is the problem everyone needs to solve.

Step 2 of 5

Prescribe a Treatment

Every player, including the Orator, secretly submits one Treatment card. The Orator reveals them all, keeping their owners anonymous.

This example is a three-player round, so three treatments are on the table.

Step 3 of 5

Add the Side Effects

Each player chooses three Side Effect cards from their hand and attaches them to the treatments.

There's one catch: you can't place more than two of your own Side Effect cards on the same treatment.

A treatment can accumulate more than two total side effects when multiple players contribute.

Shown here: five of the nine Side Effects. With three players each playing exactly three, nine land on the table in a real round.

Step 4 of 5

Vote for the Cure

Everyone, including the Orator, votes for the treatment they'd actually choose after considering its side effects.

The treatment with the most votes wins!

  • Player 1 (Orator)
  • Player 2
  • Player 3

Going to Bed gets two votes, A Haircut gets one, and Touching Grass gets none.

Step 5 of 5

Score Your Points

Submitted the winning treatment? Earn 3 points!

Added Side Effects to the winning treatment? Earn 1 point for each of your Side Effects attached to it.

  • Player 2 submitted Going to Bed+3
  • Player 3 added Dissociation to it+1
  • Player 1 (Orator) had no cards on Going to Bed+0

The two kinds of points go to whoever owns each card, so one player can earn both.
