Hamster Oracle

Tiny brain. Infinite wisdom.

Find the rules. Learn the game. Keep playing.

Hamster Oracle helps board game groups find answers, learn unfamiliar games, and keep game night moving with useful tabletop tools.

Available in Open Testing on Android.

Less Searching. More Playing.

Every board game group knows the frustration.

  1. Someone asks a rules question.
  2. The game stops.
  3. Everyone starts searching the rulebook, checking forums, or debating what the rules actually mean.

Hamster Oracle helps you spend less time searching and more time playing.

Get Answers Beyond the Rulebook

Ask board game rules questions in plain English and get clear, useful answers backed by relevant sources.

When a question goes beyond the official rulebook, Hamster Oracle can help surface community clarifications while distinguishing them from official rules.

See where an answer came from, so your group can get back to playing.

Your Board Game Rules Hub

Search for board games, save your favorites, and keep your tabletop tools close at hand.

From answering rules questions to rolling dice and keeping score, Hamster Oracle brings helpful game-night tools into one convenient place.

Learn as You Play

Learning a new board game doesn't have to mean spending the evening buried in a rulebook.

Hamster Oracle breaks complex games into guided lessons with useful explanations and quick check-ins, helping players learn the important concepts as they go.

Set Up Faster

Get from the box to the first turn with fewer headaches.

Follow step-by-step setup checklists tailored to the number of players, so your group can prepare the game without missing an important step.

Keep Every Turn on Track

Complex games have enough rules to remember.

Hamster Oracle's turn tracker helps your group follow the phases of a turn, understand what happens next, and stay organized throughout the game.

Your House Rules, Your Way

Every gaming group plays a little differently.

Save custom house rules for the games you play, so Hamster Oracle can take your group's preferences into account while distinguishing them from the official rules.

Everything Your Game Night Needs

More than a rules assistant. Hamster Oracle includes practical tools to help your group stay focused on the game.

  • Dice Roller

  • Timers

  • First-Player Picker

  • Counters

  • Score Tracker

  • Play Log
